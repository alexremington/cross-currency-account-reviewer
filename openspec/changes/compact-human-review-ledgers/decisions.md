# Decisions

- The compact ledger is a presentation/download contract, not a replacement for the private rich producer ledger.
- Account and Contact use explicit master/subordinate source-field columns for reviewability.
- Addresses are flattened into one readable column per record.
- Match statuses and rationale are concatenated rather than emitted as one column per field or diagnostic value.
- The compact contract is versioned independently from the existing rich ledger contracts.
